# Terms of Use for Private Finance Hub

Last updated: 2026-07-26

Private Finance Hub is a private, non-commercial, self-hosted application. It
is intended only for the operator's own linked financial accounts and personal
financial analysis.

The application must not be offered to third parties as a public financial
service. The operator remains responsible for protecting access credentials,
reviewing imported data, maintaining backups, and complying with the terms of
the linked financial institutions and Enable Banking.

Account information may be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable.
The application does not provide financial, tax, or investment advice.

The operator may stop using the application and revoke linked-account access at
any time.
