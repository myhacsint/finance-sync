# Privacy Notice for Private Finance Hub

Last updated: 2026-07-26

Private Finance Hub is a private, non-commercial, self-hosted application used
by its operator to retrieve and archive information from the operator's own
linked financial accounts.

## Data processed

The application may process account identifiers, balances, transaction
histories, counterparties, remittance information, and technical metadata
required for synchronization.

## Purpose and storage

Data is processed solely to provide the operator with a personal financial
archive, household analysis, and wealth overview. It is stored on systems
controlled by the operator and is not sold or used for advertising.

Enable Banking and the linked financial institutions process data as necessary
to provide account-information services under their own applicable terms and
privacy notices.

## Retention and deletion

The operator controls retention and deletion of locally stored data and
backups. Access to linked accounts can be revoked through the financial
institution or the Enable Banking control panel.

## Contact

Privacy requests should be sent to the data-protection contact address entered
by the operator when registering the Enable Banking application.
